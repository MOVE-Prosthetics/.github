# Welcome!

It is estimated that, out of the 40 million amputees living in developing countries, only around 5% have access to prosthetic devices. As a result, millions of people are left without mobility, independence, employment opportunities and education, simply because current prosthetic solutions remain too expensive or inaccessible.

At MOVE, we believe mobility should not be a privilege. Our mission is to develop durable, cost-efficient prosthetic systems through the integration of myoelectric technologies, advanced mechanical engineering, and accessible design. By focusing on affordability, modularity, and real-world functionality, we aim to create prosthetics that are not only innovative, but also practical and accessible for the communities that need them most.

MOVE is not just building prosthetics — we are engineering dreams, independence and human dignity. Join us and start changing the world.
